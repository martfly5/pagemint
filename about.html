A bookstore built around four shelves.

No algorithm deciding what you should read next — just four clear sections and ebooks worth their price.

PageMint started with a plain complaint: buying a short, useful ebook online usually means wading through a dozen unrelated titles first. We wanted one shelf for exam prep, one for people trying to earn a bit more, one for a story to fall into, and one for comics — nothing else mixed in.

Every title on PageMint is picked to earn a place on its shelf. Study ebooks are checked against real syllabi. Earning ebooks are written by people who've actually done the thing they're teaching. Story and Comics are chosen for craft, not just length. If a book doesn't clearly belong on one of the four shelves, it doesn't go up.

— The PageMint team

What we care about

One shelf, one purpose

Study, Earning, Story and Comics stay separate on purpose, so browsing one never feels like wading through the others.

Priced to actually buy

Most ebooks sit well under the price of a coffee, because a good short read shouldn't need a big decision.

Instant, always

No printing, no courier, no waiting — pay and the file is yours, in PDF or EPUB depending on the title.

How PageMint got here

2024

The idea

PageMint began as a shared folder of ebooks a few friends kept passing around, sorted into four rough piles.

2025

The first shelf

The Study shelf went live first, built for students revising in the last few weeks before exams.

2026

All four shelves

Earning, Story and Comics joined Study, and PageMint became a proper store rather than a folder.
